## 🌟 عن المشروع | About The Project

**حراج السعودية** هو أكبر سوق إعلانات مبوبة في المملكة العربية السعودية. منصة متكاملة تسمح للمستخدمين بنشر إعلاناتهم في مختلف التصنيفات مثل السيارات، العقارات، الأجهزة، الخدمات، والمزيد.

**Haraj Saudi** is the largest classified ads marketplace in Saudi Arabia. A comprehensive platform allowing users to post ads in various categories including cars, real estate, electronics, services, and more.

### ✨ المميزات | Features

- ✅ **Multi-category listings** - 16+ main categories with subcategories
- ✅ **Advanced Search** - Filter by keyword, category, location, and price
- ✅ **User Authentication** - Register, login, profile management
- ✅ **Admin Dashboard** - Complete control over posts, users, and featured listings
- ✅ **Image Upload** - Upload up to 4 images per ad
- ✅ **Pinned/Featured Posts** - Pin ads to top of homepage
- ✅ **SEO Optimized** - Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster, IndexNow
- ✅ **Arabic RTL Support** - Fully responsive Arabic interface
- ✅ **Mobile Friendly** - Sticky bottom navigation for mobile users
- ✅ **Views Counter** - Track ad popularity
- ✅ **Social Share** - Share ads on WhatsApp, Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn
- ✅ **Sitemap Generation** - Automatic sitemap.xml for search engines
